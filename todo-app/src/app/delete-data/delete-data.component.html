Data Deletion Instructions

If you wish to delete your account and all associated personal data from Tamraidee, please follow these steps:

  1. Log in to the Tamraidee application.
  2. Navigate to the Settings menu.
  3. Select Delete Account and confirm your choice.

Alternatively, you can request data deletion by sending an email to [Your Contact Email] with the subject line "Account Deletion Request." We will process your request within 7 business days.
